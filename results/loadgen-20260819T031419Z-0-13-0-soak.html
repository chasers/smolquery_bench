method

Counters are node-local and monotonic. Each pod's series is differenced between consecutive scrapes. An interval whose value falls is a pod restart and leaves every ratio it touches. An interval longer than 90 s is a missed scrape and leaves every series. Per-operation figures divide the summed time delta by the summed operation delta over the same intervals, so a slow pod carries its own weight; buckets under 20 operations are dropped. Rates sum per-pod rates rather than dividing a tier delta by a wall clock.
