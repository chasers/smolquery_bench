# OTP 27 against OTP 29, 2026-08-17

**OTP 27.3.4.6 ingests 6.2% faster than OTP 29.0.2, and it did not crash.**

Same smolquery commit (`072a26a`), same Elixir (1.20.2), same machine, same
bench. Only the OTP major differs. smolquery compiled on OTP 27 with **no source
changes**, so OTP is the single variable.

The comparison came out of [T-286](#), the SIGBUS crash. The faulting instruction
is emitted by OTP's JIT rather than written in Elixir, so an OTP swap is the
cheapest way to ask whether the bug belongs to OTP or to smolquery.

## Method

The OTP 27 build lives in a separate `git worktree`, so the OTP 29 `_build` stays
intact — switching OTP in place forces a full rebuild each way.

```sh
git worktree add --detach <path> flush-dead-band
printf 'erlang 27.3.4.6\nelixir 1.20.2-otp-27\n' > <path>/.tool-versions
# then point the bench at it
SMOLQUERY_DIR=<path> scripts/run-arm.exs smolquery
```

`mise` needs `elixir 1.20.2-otp-27` installed. smolquery declares `~> 1.20`, so
the older `1.19.5-otp-27` is rejected.

Per run: 1 VU, 10 s warm-up, 60 s measured, cold table.

## Results

| | OTP 29.0.2 | OTP 27.3.4.6 |
|---|---|---|
| runs | 4 | 6 |
| crashes | **2 of 6** (a wider set: 5 of 11) | **0 of 6** |
| mean rows/s | 43,784 | **46,512** |
| range | 43,191–44,374 | 45,780–47,147 |
| standard deviation | 520 | 463 |
| p50 | 65–67 ms | 61–63 ms |

## The throughput gap is the solid finding

**The ranges do not overlap.** The slowest OTP 27 run beats the fastest OTP 29
run. Ten runs, two tight clusters, no crossover.

Every local number this repo measured after the OTP 29 upgrade carries the
penalty. The published tables compare smolquery against ClickHouse, and a 6%
swing on one arm moves the ratio.

## The crash result is suspicious, not proven

Zero crashes in six runs is what luck alone produces with probability:

- **0.088** against the matched OTP 29 baseline of 2 in 6
- **0.026** against every OTP 29 1 VU run on 2026-08-17, 5 crashes in 11

A 3–9% coincidence. **Do not file this upstream on six runs.** Twenty runs per
side would settle it.

## Caveats

- **1 VU only.** Low-VU work is latency-bound, so the gap may not hold at 8, 16
  or 64 VU. Do not treat 6.2% as general.
- Four runs against six.
- macOS on Apple silicon. Linux may differ.

## Decision, 2026-08-17

**The next real local bench should run on OTP 27. Not switched yet.**

The evidence supports the switch — OTP 27 is faster and did not crash — but
nothing in this repo pins OTP today, and changing it mid-investigation would
invalidate the comparison the open tickets rest on. Make the switch a deliberate
step, with its own re-baseline, rather than a side effect.

When you do switch:

1. Repeat at 8, 16 and 64 VU first, to learn whether 6.2% is general or a
   low-VU artifact.
2. Re-baseline both arms on OTP 27. Do not mix OTP 27 and OTP 29 numbers in one
   table.
3. Say which OTP produced every published figure. Every result before this date
   is OTP 29.
